What Is Raw Data?

Raw data is the information collected directly during the web scraping stage. At this stage, the data has not yet gone through the complete cleaning and transformation process. These files serve as the original input for the later stages of the project.

Data Characteristics

The raw datasets contain information collected from Formula 1 web sources. They form the starting point for the project's data-processing pipeline.

  • Technical specifications of Formula 1 cars
  • Team and driver performance information
  • Race-result information
  • Constructor/team standings
  • Race and win-related information
