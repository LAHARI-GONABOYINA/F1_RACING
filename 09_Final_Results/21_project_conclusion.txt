The project successfully demonstrates a web-based approach for collecting Formula 1 technical specification and performance data. The analysis shows that McLaren's performance improved substantially from 2024 to 2025, with championship points increasing by 25.08% and race wins increasing by 133.33%. At the same time, only a limited number of extracted numerical technical specifications changed. Therefore, the results identify an observed relationship between the available technical data and racing performance, but they do not establish causation. A larger dataset containing more teams, seasons, and technical variables would provide a stronger basis for statistical analysis.
