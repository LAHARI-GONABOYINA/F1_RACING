PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

Analysis of Formula 1 race performance, points, wins and team results

What We Did

The performance analysis stage focuses on analyzing Formula 1 team and race performance using the data collected through web scraping. The analysis includes championship points, race wins and season-to-season team performance.

Performance Analysis Process

01. Collect Performance Data

Race results, team standings and other performance information were collected from Formula 1 web sources.

02. Prepare Race Results

The scraped race results were organized into a structured format for further analysis.

03. Analyze Team Points

Constructor championship points were compared across the selected seasons.

04. Analyze Race Wins

The number of race victories was compared to evaluate changes in team performance.

05. Compare Teams

McLaren and Mercedes performance was compared across the 2024 and 2025 seasons.

06. Visualize Results

Performance trends and comparisons were represented using charts and graphs.

McLaren Performance Change

McLaren's championship points increased from 666 points in 2024 to 833 points in 2025.

This represents an increase of approximately 25.08%.

McLaren race wins increased from 6 wins in 2024 to 14 wins in 2025.

This represents an increase of approximately 133.33%.

Mercedes Performance

Mercedes recorded 468 points in 2024 and 469 points in 2025.

The points changed only slightly between the two seasons.

Mercedes recorded 4 wins in 2024 and 2 wins in 2025, showing a decrease in race victories.

Performance Findings

McLaren: The team remained first in the constructor standings in both analyzed seasons.

McLaren's points increased by approximately 25.08% from 2024 to 2025.

McLaren's race wins increased by approximately 133.33%.

Mercedes: Championship points remained almost unchanged, increasing from 468 to 469.

The comparison shows a substantial improvement in McLaren's measured performance between the two seasons.
